## Goal

Design a portfolio of 1–3 Instagram accounts targeting new and expecting
parents, focused on young child development (0–3 years) and postpartum
well-being. The accounts should be buildable by a single creator without
professional video production, and reach a monetisation milestone
(first recurring revenue stream) within 6–9 months of consistent posting.

## Context & Constraints

- The creator is a mother with first-hand postpartum and early-parenting
  experience but no clinical credential (not a doctor, nurse, or therapist).
  Content must stay clearly in the "lived experience + curated evidence"
  lane and avoid medical advice.
- Budget for tools, props, and software is under €50/month at launch.
- Content will be produced in French initially, with possible English
  expansion later.
- The creator can commit roughly 10–12 hours per week to content
  creation, community management, and learning.
- Instagram is the primary platform; repurposing to TikTok or YouTube
  Shorts is welcome but not required in the initial plan.

## What to Explore

1. **Account theming & positioning** — Should there be one broad account
   or 2–3 tightly niched accounts (e.g., one for postpartum recovery
   mindset, one for baby sensory/motor milestones, one for
   parent-friendly meal prep)? What positioning avoids the most
   saturated pockets while still having search demand?

2. **Content formats & cadence** — Reels vs. carousels vs. Stories
   series. What mix maximises reach-per-hour-invested for a solo
   creator? What posting cadence is sustainable at 10–12 h/week?

3. **Monetisation pathways** — Rank realistic revenue streams:
   affiliate links (baby products, books, apps), digital products
   (printable milestone trackers, routine planners, mini e-books),
   paid community or membership, UGC/brand partnerships, sponsored
   posts. Which are reachable earliest and which scale best?

4. **Growth tactics** — Collaborations, hashtag strategy, SEO-style
   caption writing, giveaway loops, email list building from day one.
   What is the highest-leverage growth lever in the first 90 days?

5. **Risk & failure modes** — Content burnout at 10 h/week, algorithm
   dependency, audience trust erosion if a post edges into medical
   advice territory, saturation in the "mom influencer" space, French-
   language market size limits. How should the strategy hedge against
   each?

## Evaluation Criteria (what makes one strategy better than another)

- **Time-to-first-revenue**: shorter is better.
- **Sustainable effort**: must stay within the weekly hour budget
  without grinding the creator down.
- **Defensibility**: strategies that build owned assets (email list,
  digital product catalogue, recognisable brand) score higher than
  those dependent purely on algorithmic reach.
- **Trust safety**: strategies that keep the creator clearly out of
  the medical-advice zone score higher.
- **Upside optionality**: strategies that open doors to adjacent
  revenue (courses, coaching, book deals, brand ambassadorships)
  score higher than dead-end paths.
